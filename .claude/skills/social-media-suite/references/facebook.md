# Facebook

**Limits:** post up to 63,206 chars, but keep it to 40-80 words for reach; about 480 chars show
before "See more". Page "About" intro 255 chars.

**Sizes:** feed image 1080×1350 (4:5) or 1080×1080; link preview 1200×630; Reels and Stories
1080×1920; cover photo 851×315 (keep the safe area centred for mobile).

**What works**
- Community and conversation: questions, polls, "this or that", local news, behind the scenes.
- Groups: value-first posts. Follow group rules; no hard selling.
- Reels get the widest organic reach; reuse IG Reels natively, without the watermark.
- Links can go directly in the post (unlike IG). Use a clear preview image and headline.
- Events for launches and workshops; Offers for promotions.

**Post structure:** short hook → 1-3 short lines → question or CTA → 1-3 hashtags (optional;
less important on FB).

**Ads note:** for boosted posts, hand off to `agency-performance-marketer`. Avoid "you"
statements about personal attributes (health, finances, ethnicity), as they breach Meta ad policy.

**Messenger / comment replies:** respond within hours; set up auto-replies for FAQs (price,
location, opening hours).

**Best times:** weekdays 9am to 1pm local; test against Page Insights.
