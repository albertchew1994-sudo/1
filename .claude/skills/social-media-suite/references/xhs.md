# Xiaohongshu (小红书 / XHS / RedNote)

Write in **Simplified Chinese** by default. The audience is mostly young, urban women aged 18 to
35, but categories like tech, career, travel and finance are growing. XHS works as a search engine
plus a 种草 (product discovery) community, so posts need to be authentic, useful and
keyword-rich.

**Limits:** title ≤ 20 characters; body ≤ 1,000 characters; up to 18 images; video ideally
under 5 minutes. Topic tags (#话题) are usually 5-10.

**Sizes:** 3:4 vertical (1080×1440) is best for images; 9:16 for video. The cover image decides
the click, so use big bold Chinese text with a high-contrast background or a real-life photo.

**Title formulas (标题)**
- Number + result: 「3个月涨粉1万｜我做对了这5件事」
- Pain + solution: 「毛孔粗大救星😭 黄皮亲测有效」
- Identity + scene: 「打工人必备｜10分钟搞定一周便当」
- Contrast or surprise: 「花了2000块才明白…」
- Use emojis and the separator 「｜」; put the search keyword near the front.

**Body structure (正文)**
```
开头: 一句共鸣/痛点 + 结论先行 (emoji)
干货: 1️⃣ 2️⃣ 3️⃣ 分点，每点1-3行，多用emoji分隔
个人体验: 真实细节 (时间、价格、使用感受、前后对比)
结尾: 互动提问 (“你们还有什么好方法？评论区告诉我👇”) + 收藏提示
#话题标签 ×5-10 (大词+长尾词，如 #护肤 #油皮护肤 #平价好物)
```

**Rules and compliance (important)**
- No external links, QR codes, phone numbers or WeChat IDs in posts; these risk 限流 (throttling).
  Drive traffic via the profile, 群聊 or the official store.
- Avoid absolute advertising words: 最, 第一, 顶级, 100%, 国家级, 绝对.
- No medical or efficacy claims (治疗, 根治) and no financial return promises.
- Sponsored content must use 蒲公英 (Pugongying) brand collaboration, or it risks penalties.
- Don't copy content from other platforms with watermarks.

**Content types that perform:** 干货合集/清单, 测评对比, 教程, before/after, 好物分享,
vlog/日常, 避坑指南.

**Tips:** post at 12 to 14 and 19 to 22 (China time); reply to comments in the first hour;
keep a consistent cover style for a recognisable grid; do keyword research through XHS
search suggestions (下拉词) and related searches.

**Profile:** nickname = name + niche keyword; bio = who you are + what you share + update
frequency; no contact details.
