# LinkedIn

**Limits:** post 3,000 chars; about 210 chars show before "…see more". Headline 220. About 2,600.
Comments 1,250. Document (PDF carousel) up to 300 pages / 100 MB. Video up to 10 min (native).

**Formats that work:** text plus single image; PDF carousel (8-12 slides, 1080×1350 or 1080×1080);
native video (vertical 4:5 or 9:16, captions on); polls (sparingly).

**Post structure**
1. Hook line (≤ 12 words): a bold claim, a number, a short story opener, or a contrarian take.
2. A blank line, then short 1-2 sentence paragraphs with plenty of white space.
3. Value: a story → a lesson, or a numbered framework.
4. A takeaway line.
5. CTA: a question to drive comments, or "Follow for…" / "Repost ♻️ if…".
6. 3-5 hashtags at the end (mix of broad and niche).

**Tips**
- First person and specific. Avoid corporate buzzwords.
- Put external links in the first comment or accept reduced reach; say which in the draft.
- Tag people or companies only when they're genuinely relevant.
- Best times (B2B): Tue to Thu, 8 to 10am and 12pm, in the local time zone of the audience.
- Company page vs personal profile: personal posts usually get far more reach, so suggest the
  founder or employees post.

**Profile:** headline = who you help + how + proof. Banner = value proposition + CTA. Featured =
lead magnet, best post, website.
